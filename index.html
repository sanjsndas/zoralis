    Frequently asked questions

    What is the best soil mix for raised beds?

    A blend of 40% compost, 30% topsoil, and 30% aeration (coco coir or pumice) gives excellent structure and nutrition.

    How often should I water deep-rooted perennials?

    Deep watering once a week during dry spells is better than light daily watering — it encourages deep roots.

    Can I grow vegetables in partial shade?

    Yes. Leafy greens, herbs, and root vegetables like beets and carrots tolerate 4-5 hours of direct sun.
