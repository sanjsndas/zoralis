🍪 Cookies Policy

We use cookies to enhance your experience, remember preferences, and gather anonymous usage data. Your privacy is our priority.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help us remember your preferences, understand how you interact with our content, and improve your overall experience. We use only minimal, privacy‑conscious cookies.

How we use cookies

Zoralis Green Pathways uses cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential functionality – to keep the website working properly (e.g., remembering your cookie consent choice).
  • Preferences – to remember your language or region settings.
  • Analytics – to collect anonymous statistics about page visits and traffic sources, helping us improve our content.
🌿 We do not use tracking cookies for advertising or share your data with third‑party advertisers. Your browsing remains yours.

Cookies we use

Below is a list of the cookies we typically place on your device. We keep this list updated, but please note that third‑party services (like embedded videos) may set their own cookies.

Name Purpose Duration
zoralis_consent Stores your cookie consent preference. 1 year
zoralis_session Maintains session state for logged‑in users (if applicable). Session

_gid (Google Analytics) Distinguishes users for analytics. 24 hours

Managing your cookies

You can control and delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block or remove cookies, though this may affect the functionality of certain features on our site.

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data.
  • Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data.
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data.
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies.

Third‑party cookies

We sometimes embed content from external platforms (e.g., gardening videos, research articles). These services may place their own cookies. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies for more information.

Your consent

By continuing to use our website, you agree to our use of cookies as described in this policy. You can withdraw your consent at any time by adjusting your browser settings or clearing your cookies.

Updates to this policy

We may update this Cookies Policy to reflect changes in technology or legal requirements. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
